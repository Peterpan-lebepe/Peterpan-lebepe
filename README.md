## 👋 About Me

I work at the intersection of data, compliance, and reporting within media operations — making sure high-volume campaign data is accurate, consistent, and ready for decisions.

Day to day, that means validating and analysing campaign performance data, tracking down discrepancies, and producing reporting that internal teams and clients actually rely on. Over time this has pulled me into quality control, stakeholder coordination, and more data-driven decision support — which is what pointed me toward analytics in the first place.

I'm currently building out my technical toolkit — SQL, Power BI, and Python — through a Data Analytics Bootcamp, and applying what I'm learning to real datasets rather than just exercises. The goal isn't a career pivot so much as a natural extension: nine-plus years of knowing what "trustworthy data" looks like, now paired with the tools to build and visualise it myself.

**Core strengths:** data validation, reporting accuracy, campaign analytics, operational data quality.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛒 [NovaRetail SQL Analysis](https://github.com/Peterpan-lebepe/NovaRetail-SQL-Analysis)
A SQL and BI project analysing retail transactional data in Databricks — turning raw sales and customer records into business-relevant insights.

Covers data exploration, filtering and aggregation (COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX), retail customer and sales analysis, and schema interpretation, built end-to-end as a Databricks SQL notebook.

**Stack:** SQL · Databricks · CSV · Relational Data

---

### 📊 [Advertising Campaign Performance Dashboard](https://github.com/Peterpan-lebepe/advertising-campaign-excel-dashboard)
A multi-sheet Excel dashboard built on a 3,600+ row advertising dataset, with live SUMIFS/COUNTIFS formulas, KPI cards, and four supporting charts — designed to answer real business questions about campaign performance, not just display numbers.

Covers KPI tracking, campaign performance breakdowns by channel and date range, and pivot-table-driven summaries — built to surface trends a manager or client could act on directly.

**Stack:** Excel · Pivot Tables · Dashboard Design · Data Cleaning
